Spot the concern. Act before serving.

MBG kitchens pack hundreds of trays before dawn. Staff photograph each table with a phone; AI agents ring the food that looks wrong, and staff fix it before the lids go on.

One photo, four hands

  1. StaffSnap each table or rack as it is packed. Messy is fine: any angle, any count.
  2. Vision + rules agentsRing every bad-looking spot, check it against the day's menu and the food-safety rules.
  3. StaffFix and retake, or dismiss with a reason. Never a tray count, never an auto-clear.
  4. SupervisorSees every safety finding, how fast it was fixed, and can hold a batch.
